Services

What the shop does.

Every job uses professional, pH-balanced products, the two-bucket wash method and microfiber only. Nothing that'll scratch your paint to save ten minutes.

Interior detail

A deep vacuum, steam cleaning, and leather and fabric work for the stains a vacuum won't touch. Pet hair included. Kry has seen worse.

Starting at $50

Exterior detail

A paint-safe hand wash, then decontamination to pull embedded grit out of the clear coat. Tires and trim get dressed before it goes home.

Starting at $50

Paint correction

Machine polishing that takes out swirl marks and light scratches, so the paint looks deep and clear again instead of hazy in the sun.

Heavy correction from $350

Ceramic coating

Long-term protection bonded to the paint. Water beads up and rolls off, dirt has a harder time sticking, and washing gets a lot easier.

Starting at $1,500

Headlight restoration

Yellowed, cloudy lenses brought back to clear. It looks better, and you'll actually see the road at night.

Both headlights $100

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Something else on your mind? Call 269-391-6489.

How long does a full detail take?

Most interior and exterior details take 3 to 5 hours. Paint correction and ceramic coating can run a full day. You'll get a clear timeline when you book.

Where are you located?

It's a drop-off shop in the Kalamazoo area. Customers come from Portage, Oshtemo, Allegan, Plainwell, Gobles, Mattawan, Paw Paw and the rest of Southwest Michigan. Call 269-391-6489 for directions.

How much will mine cost?

The pricing above gives you starting points. Every vehicle gets inspected before work starts, and the final price depends on size and condition. Call for a quick, honest quote.

Are the products safe for my paint and interior?

Yes. Everything is professional grade and pH-balanced. The wash is two-bucket with microfiber only, and polishing is done carefully, never by hacking at the paint with a rotary.

How often should I get it detailed?

For a daily driver, a full detail every 3 to 4 months keeps it in good shape. With a ceramic coating you can go longer between details, and upkeep is easier.
